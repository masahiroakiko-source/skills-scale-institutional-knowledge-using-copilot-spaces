# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers help teams execute consistently by coordinating work, dependencies, and delivery rhythm across project functions. They keep execution aligned with plans while helping remove blockers and improve team focus.

### Responsibilities
- Sequence work across teams and milestones to support delivery goals
- Monitor dependencies, capacity, and delivery health
- Coordinate cross-functional handoffs and escalation paths
- Maintain visibility into blockers, decisions, and next steps
- Support project leads in balancing scope, resourcing, and risk

### Goals
- Keep delivery moving without unnecessary delay or churn
- Improve predictability across project milestones
- Strengthen collaboration between engineering, product, and project stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly delivery reviews and dependency check-ins
- Status updates for project leadership and stakeholders
- Coordination with project managers, product managers, and engineering leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align schedule, milestones, and stakeholder communication
- Collaborates with Developers to understand delivery constraints and workload priorities
- Partners with Product Managers to reconcile roadmap commitments with team capacity
- Raises cross-team blockers early so the Project Manager and stakeholders can respond quickly

---

## Risk and Dependency Leads

### Role Summary
Risk and Dependency Leads identify, track, and escalate the factors that could disrupt delivery. They help teams anticipate issues before they become blockers and manage dependencies across teams or stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain a visible register of risks, assumptions, and dependencies
- Assess impact, urgency, and ownership for each issue
- Coordinate mitigation plans and contingency actions
- Escalate unresolved issues to the right decision-makers or sponsors
- Support teams in proactive communication when dependencies slip

### Goals
- Reduce surprises and avoid unnecessary delivery disruption
- Ensure issues are visible and addressed early
- Improve decision-making with clear risk context and ownership

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and escalation updates
- Dependency tracking across workstreams and stakeholder groups
- Briefings for project leads and executive stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on the project risk register and mitigation plans
- Collaborates with Developers to identify technical dependencies and delivery risks
- Provides Product Managers with insight into trade-offs, customer impact, and decision urgency
- Keeps Delivery Managers and stakeholders informed about blockers that may affect timeline or outcomes

---

## Product and Customer Advocates

### Role Summary
Product and Customer Advocates ensure the work remains grounded in user needs, business value, and stakeholder priorities. They help teams validate that the solution meets the intended purpose and supports meaningful outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer, user, and stakeholder perspectives in planning and reviews
- Clarify desired outcomes, usage scenarios, and success metrics
- Validate assumptions through feedback, research, and examples
- Help prioritize issues based on customer value and business impact
- Ensure decisions are informed by outcomes rather than only feature delivery

### Goals
- Protect customer value and business relevance
- Improve clarity around what success looks like for the work
- Support stronger decision-making with user and stakeholder context

### Typical Communication
- Feedback sessions, demos, and stakeholder interviews
- Prioritization discussions with product and project leads
- Reviews of acceptance criteria and outcome validation plans

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define priorities, adoption goals, and customer impact
- Feeds insight to Developers and Project Managers so work is aligned with real user needs
- Helps Delivery Managers understand what changes matter most when trade-offs are needed
- Provides context to stakeholder groups so they can assess whether project outcomes match expectations

---

## Quality and Release Partners

### Role Summary
Quality and Release Partners help ensure that work is ready, validated, and deployable. They connect quality practices, release readiness, and operational stability with the broader project plan.

### Responsibilities
- Support testing strategies, validation plans, and release readiness reviews
- Coordinate release criteria, deployment checks, and rollback planning
- Help identify quality risks, operational concerns, and readiness gaps
- Collaborate with engineering and project leaders on launch readiness
- Ensure handoff from delivery to release and operations is clear and documented

### Goals
- Improve confidence in release quality and operational readiness
- Reduce avoidable defects, regressions, and deployment issues
- Ensure stakeholder expectations match the actual release state

### Typical Communication
- Quality gates, readiness reviews, and release checklists
- Coordination with project managers, developers, and leadership
- Deployment readiness calls and operational handoff briefings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to verify readiness, test coverage, and release criteria
- Supports Project Managers with schedule confidence and risk awareness around launch timing
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm the product is ready to meet user needs and business goals
- Coordinates with Delivery Managers to align release sequencing with project milestones and dependencies
